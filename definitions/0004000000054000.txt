#            Byte 0 is the status: 01 = open (also in unused files),
#            02 = beaten. Byte 1 holds per-run flags (star medals).
#            1-3, the castle and the Toad House use a different block that
#            isn't mapped yet.
#   +0x2B7B  NOT usable: number of the last spot beaten (went 7 -> 6 when
#            the Toad House was beaten after the castle).
